A LETTER · FROM THE HEART14 NOVEMBER

If I hurt you, I'm sorry. Amar kokhono intention chilo na tomake hurt korar. Ar sotti bolte, you were the one who used to make my days enjoyable. Jar sathe mon khule kotha bolte partam, jar sathe kotha bole sob kharap din-o ektu valo hoye jeto. But akhon 🙂 kichu bolar nei.

Honestly, sobtheke baje din gulo katachhi ekhon. Onek karon ache tomar sathe kotha bolar. Tomar sathe kotha bondho howar next day thekei jor, ekhono thik hoyni. Pray 8 din hoye gelo, temperature 100°F-er nichei namche na. Tobuo ami sarata somoy wait kore gechi, ei ashay je hoyto tomar ekta message ashbe, at least ekta last message.

But tumi amake opekkhay rekhei chole gele. And the funny thing is, tumi notun ID baniye nile amake avoid korar jonno? Literally? Amake avoid kora eto kothin chilo? Just ekbar last kotha bole block kore dite parte. Sobai jemon chole jay, tumio chole jete parte. Kintu evabe notun ID bananor ki dorkar chilo? Ekta last message toh kortei parte, tai na?

Ami ki sotti eto tai kharap chilam?

Just leave it. Ar bolbo na je amake message koro, reply dao. Because jaane wale ko kaun hi rok sakta hai?

Tobuo opekkha thakbe. Oi ID-ta ekhono thakbe. Jodi konodin tomar mon hoy, jodi kokhono mone hoy amar sathe kotha bolbe, message korte paro. Available thakbo, jodi beche thaki.

Take care of yourself. And thank you for making some of my days beautiful.
